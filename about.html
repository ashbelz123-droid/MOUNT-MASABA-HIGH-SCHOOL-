ABOUT MOUNT MASABA HIGH SCHOOL

School History

Mount Masaba High School was established in 2000 with 53 students in rented premises. Since then, it has grown into one of the leading secondary schools in Mbale, expanding its campus, introducing modern learning facilities, building international partnerships, and achieving strong academic performance.

School Milestones

2000 - School opened with 53 students. Hosted Prof. Nancy Dorsey (USA). Recorded a 72% UCE pass rate. Acquired five acres of land in Mbale Municipality.
2001 - Started the needy students' bursary scheme. Sent the first pioneer students to university.
2002 - Hosted Professors Richard and Marjorie McIntosh. Donated scholastic materials to Busamaga Primary School. Sent 25 students to university.
2003 - Received computers and science equipment. Launched the ICT Centre. Hosted Hon. Michael Werikhe Kafabusa.
2004 - Started the Professor McIntosh Scholarship Scheme. Achieved a 96% UCE pass rate.
2005 - Launched the Alumni Association. Began construction of the first classroom block. Ranked among the top five schools in Mbale District in UACE.
2006 - Started the IT Essentials Programme with Makerere University. Sent 50 students to university. Launched the solar power project.
2007 - Officially opened the current campus. Opened the CISCO Academy. Began construction of the second classroom block.
2008 - Received computer donations from Uganda Telecom Limited. Won the Mbale District Rugby Trophy. Started the rainwater harvesting project. Achieved a 100% UCE pass rate.
2009 - Hosted Hon. Rukiya Chekamondo. Started construction of the girls' dormitory.
2010 - Fully relocated to the current campus.
2011 - Hosted the first international volunteer teacher from Sweden.
2012 - Introduced the lunch programme for day students.
2013 - Constructed a three-classroom storied block.
2014 - Achieved a 100% UCE pass rate.
2015 - Acquired a school truck.
