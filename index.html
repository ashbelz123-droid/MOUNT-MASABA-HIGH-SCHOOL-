About Us

Mount Masaba High School is a leading private O-Level and A-Level mixed day and boarding secondary school located in Mbale District, Uganda. The school was founded in 2000 and is registered by the Ministry of Education, Science, Technology and Sports and the Uganda National Examinations Board (UNEB).

Vision

Our vision is to create a sustainably educated, empowered and morally upright community.

Mission

The school mission is to promote excellence, integrity, teamwork and partnerships at all times.
