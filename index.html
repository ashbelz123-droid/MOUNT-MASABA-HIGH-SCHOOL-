Welcome to Mount Masaba

A place to learn.
A place to become.

Mount Masaba High School is built around a simple belief: education should prepare students not only for examinations, but for life.

Through purposeful learning, responsible leadership, discipline and a supportive school community, we strive to bring out the best in every learner.
